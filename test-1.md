# Kurti.app — Executive Brief

## Situation

Kurti.app is positioned as a **fashion commerce and creator-business platform**, combining retail, wholesale/reselling, creator content, fit feedback, and designer/tailor collaboration. Its broader proposition is to guide users through an **8-step fashion-business journey** from trend discovery and sampling through manufacturing, marketing, sales, fulfillment, and learning. 

The platform already describes several monetization paths: reseller/shop owner, creator/influencer, fit-checker, and designer/tailor. 

## Key Findings

* **Multiple revenue/user roles:** Reselling is currently supported through wholesale pricing and fulfillment; creators receive 10% discount coupons, with cash commissions described as a future capability. 
* **Strong creator/community layer:** Users can upload looks, participate in social discovery, and provide photo-based fit reviews. 
* **End-to-end business proposition:** The 8-step framework provides a coherent path from discovery → sampling → production → promotion → selling → fulfillment → learning, with stated 1:1 assistance. 
* **Commerce incentives are clearly defined:** The document lists a first-order 10% coupon, free delivery above NPR 999, COD nationwide, and creator coupons. 
* **PWA/mobile accessibility:** Kurti.app is presented as a Progressive Web App installable from mobile browsers. 
* **Customer/social proof is emphasized:** The document reports a 4.7★ average rating and highlights photo-based customer feedback, including fit, color, fabric, and delivery experiences. 

## Key Risks / Gaps

1. **Monetization maturity:** Direct creator cash commissions are still described as forthcoming, so the creator-economy proposition may currently rely more on discounts and engagement than direct earnings. 
2. **Returns/refunds are under-specified:** The source explicitly says published return/refund terms are not detailed. This is a material customer-confidence and operational risk for ecommerce. 
3. **Operational complexity:** Supporting retail, wholesale, dropshipping, creators, fit-checkers, sampling, manufacturing, and designer collaboration creates significant workflow and fulfillment complexity.
4. **Claims need operational validation:** Pricing, discounts, ratings, delivery, COD, coupons, and future rewards should be kept synchronized with actual platform behavior and current catalog data.
5. **Conversion dependency on trust:** The platform's model depends heavily on authentic looks, reviews, fit information, and customer proof; weak participation could reduce the value of the marketplace/community loop.

## Recommended Next Steps

### Immediate — Product & Trust

* Publish a **clear return/refund policy** with eligibility, timelines, process, and responsibility.
* Audit every documented offer—`KURTI10`, free shipping threshold, creator coupons, COD—and ensure frontend/backend behavior matches.
* Establish a single source of truth for **catalog prices, discounts, inventory, and promotional messaging**.
* Track the complete purchase funnel: visit → product/look → cart → checkout → payment/COD → delivery → review.

### Near Term — Growth

* Formalize the **creator → look → engagement → sale → reward** loop before expanding creator cash commissions.
* Strengthen photo reviews and fit-checker content as a core conversion mechanism.
* Develop reseller onboarding around the existing 8-step framework and measure reseller activation, repeat orders, and margins.

### Strategic — Platform

* Treat Kurti.app as more than a storefront: build around the combined **commerce + creator + community + business-enablement** model.
* Prioritize workflows that connect community content directly to commerce.
* Define measurable KPIs for each business model rather than treating the entire platform as one funnel.

**Executive takeaway:** The source presents Kurti.app as an emerging **fashion-commerce ecosystem rather than simply an online kurti store**. Its strongest strategic asset is the integration of commerce, creator content, community feedback, and guided fashion entrepreneurship; the immediate priorities are tightening operational trust, validating documented claims against actual platform behavior, and turning creator/community activity into measurable commercial outcomes.
